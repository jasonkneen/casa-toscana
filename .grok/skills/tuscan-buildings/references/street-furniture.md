# Street furniture & openings (from Lucca / Prato photos)

Photographs of real piano-terra: two-tone walls, barred windows, rusticated
arches, green plank doors, gas boxes, pipes.

## Two-tone wall
- **Dado**: grey roughcast / cement, ~1.15–1.30 m, wraps the street face.
- **Field**: ochre / peach / cream stucco above.
- Barred windows sit **just above** the dado line, not in it.
- Doors cut through the dado; stone jambs sit proud of it.

## Barred windows
- Deep stucco rebate, little or no stone surround.
- Tight iron grid, 5×5 or 6×5, slightly rusted or galvanized grey.
- Dark wood casement **behind** the bars.
- High sill (~1.65–1.75 m). Interior visible through the grid.

## Arch doors
Three families, pick one per house:

1. **Walnut androne** — rusticated pietra serena voussoirs, paneled double
   walnut leaves, iron kick plate, **fanlight** with radiating bars or
   scrolls, brass knobs, doorbell to one side.
2. **Green plank arch** — same rusticated stone, vertical green boards,
   peeling paint, small step, house number + intercoms.
3. **Herringbone / chevron** — green (or weathered teal) boards in a
   chevron, studs, kickboard, simple stone arch (sometimes no rustication).

## Other street doors
- **Carriage / passo carrabile** — wide green vertical planks, no arch,
  kickboard, often a no-parking plate.
- **Shop arch** — iron gate, open during the day, next to a narrow stone
  stair door (B&B / flats).
- **Teal french** — glass leaves with diamond lattice security grille.

## Furniture that makes it a street
- Galvanized **gas meter** box + conduit elbows
- Cream or brown **downpipe**
- Octagonal **mailbox** on a post
- White enamel **house number**
- Intercom / doorbell cluster
- Never leave a blank piano-terra bay
